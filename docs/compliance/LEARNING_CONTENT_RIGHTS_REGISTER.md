# Learning Content Rights Register

Status: working register — future content remains gated  
Owner: Chijindu Oreh  
Last reviewed: 12 September 2026

This register records the source and permitted use of learner-visible content.
An item must not be imported into production or published merely because Study
Buddy possesses a copy. The evidence itself belongs in the restricted company
legal store; this repository should contain only a non-confidential reference.

## Current scope

| Content class | Current user-visible position | Rights position | Evidence/action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Study Buddy explanations | Visible where used by the current service | Co-founders confirm Study Buddy has the rights to use and publish them | Preserve creation/source records and add item-level references as the catalogue is formalised |
| Textbooks or textbook extracts | Not displayed | No publication right recorded | Keep disabled until owner permission or another verified lawful basis covers the exact use |
| Genuine past examination questions, examiner reports, and marking materials | Not displayed or represented as genuine past questions | No WAEC licence, permission, partnership, affiliation, approval, or endorsement | Keep disabled until the appropriate rights are documented; do not imply an official relationship |
| Contractor spreadsheet and derived material | Not approved for production import or publication | Contractor has not signed an assignment or licence | Chijindu Oreh must obtain the signed agreement and complete the item-level third-party rights review first |

## Required fields for every future resource

- internal resource identifier and title;
- creator, publisher, examination body, or other source;
- whether the material is original, licensed, public-domain, or used under a
  separately reviewed legal basis;
- exact permitted uses, territory, term, adaptation rights, AI/retrieval rights,
  commercial rights, and sublicensing restrictions;
- executed agreement, permission, or other evidence reference and storage
  location;
- embedded third-party material and its separate rights evidence;
- review owner, review date, expiry/renewal date, and approval state; and
- required attribution, notices, withdrawal steps, and dispute history.

## Publication rule

Only an approved resource with complete evidence may become learner-visible.
A contractor assignment covers only rights the contractor owns; it does not
make copied WAEC questions, textbook extracts, examiner materials, or other
third-party works safe to publish. Labels such as “past question”, “official”,
“WAEC”, or similar provenance claims require evidence matching that exact claim.
