# School Data Processing Schedule

Status: contract schedule template — not legal advice and not executed  
Version: 0.2, 8 September 2026

Launch decision recorded 10 September 2026: the current product provides no
school or teacher accounts and no school-staff access to individual student
data. This template applies only to a possible future school service and must
not be treated as permission to enable access.

This schedule is intended to attach to a signed service agreement between the
Study Buddy contracting entity (“Study Buddy”) and a named school (“School”). A
qualified Nigerian lawyer/privacy adviser must adapt and approve it. Do not use
the template until the legal entities, roles, services, data fields, school
visibility, retention, pricing, and liability terms are completed.

## 4. School instructions and responsibilities

The School will:

- provide only lawful, accurate, necessary data and authorised instructions;
- identify the lawful basis and any required parent/guardian authorisation;
- provide students and families clear information before enrolment;
- maintain an up-to-date list of authorised staff and promptly remove leavers;
- assign roles according to least privilege and keep credentials confidential;
- avoid placing health, safeguarding, or other sensitive records in free-text or
  AI fields unless expressly approved;
- tell Study Buddy promptly about corrections, withdrawals, objections,
  departures, incidents, and rights requests; and
- use learning analytics as support, not as the sole basis for a high-stakes
  disciplinary, grading, admission, or welfare decision.

## 5. Study Buddy obligations

Study Buddy will, for processor activities:

- act only on documented lawful instructions;
- ensure authorised personnel have confidentiality duties;
- apply appropriate technical and organisational security;
- restrict school users to approved students, fields, and purposes;
- assist with rights requests, DPIAs, consultation, breaches, and compliance;
- maintain processor/sub-processor and processing records as required;
- return or delete data at the end of service according to Section 10; and
- provide information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance.

Study Buddy will immediately flag an instruction it reasonably believes violates
data-protection law and may pause affected processing while the issue is resolved.

## 7. Security

Minimum measures include strong authentication, least privilege, tenant/school
isolation, encrypted transport, secure secrets, rate limiting, logging of
administrative access, secure development/review, backups, vulnerability and
dependency management, incident response, and tested deletion. The final
agreement should attach the approved security schedule without disclosing
details that would weaken security.

## 8. Sub-processors and international transfers

Attach the approved providers from the Processor and Vendor Register, their
service, location, and transfer mechanism. Define advance notice of a new
sub-processor and a reasonable objection/escalation process. Study Buddy remains
responsible for processor obligations it delegates as required by law and
contract.

## 9. Rights requests and complaints

- Each party will forward a request relevant to the other without undue delay.
- The role matrix determines who responds, but the student must not be sent back
  and forth without ownership.
- Identity and parental/school authority must be proportionately verified.
- Study Buddy will not respond on the School’s behalf for processor data unless
  instructed or legally required.
- Maintain request, decision, response, and deadline records.

## 10. Retention, return and deletion

On school exit or agreement termination, the parties must identify data to:

1. return securely to the School;
2. transfer to a direct student account only with a lawful basis and clear choice;
3. delete from active systems within the approved target;
4. age out of backups within the approved recovery window; or
5. retain under a specific documented legal obligation or dispute hold.

The signed schedule must insert exact periods from the approved Data Retention
and Deletion Schedule. Study Buddy should provide completion evidence that does
not expose other users or confidential security information.

## 11. Incidents

Study Buddy will notify the School without undue delay after becoming aware of a
personal-data breach affecting School processor data and provide available
details needed for risk assessment and notification. The parties will preserve
evidence, coordinate accurate communications, and avoid delaying a legally
required regulator or student/parent notice.

Insert contractual contacts, maximum initial-notice target, update frequency,
decision ownership, and responsibility for direct notification.

## 12. Audit and assistance

Use proportionate evidence such as independent reports, policies, test results,
and written responses before intrusive inspection. An on-site audit should be
limited to justified material concerns, reasonable notice, confidentiality, and
protection of other customers and security. Urgent incident/regulator rights are
not restricted by this process.
