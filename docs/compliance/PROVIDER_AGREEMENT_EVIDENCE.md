## Completion procedure

For each active provider, an authorised founder must verify the account legal
name, accept or execute the applicable agreement, and save dated acceptance
evidence in the same private evidence store. Record the acceptance date,
contracting provider entity, document version, account or organisation ID, and
review owner in the processor register. A certified DPO must then validate the
processor role and transfer treatment. Never commit signed agreements, account
screenshots, customer IDs, or other confidential evidence to this repository.
